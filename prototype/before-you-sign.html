    What this is

    A prototype to help you understand a product before you commit. Figures are estimates from what you enter, using standard repayment maths. They will differ from the provider's exact figures.

    What this is not

    Not financial advice, and not connected to any bank. It does not check your credit, store your data or make payments. Always read the provider's own documents.
